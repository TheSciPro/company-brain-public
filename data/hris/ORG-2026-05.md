3. **Rahul Bhatia** (NW0011) becomes Deputy Head, Consumer (India), reporting to Eleanor Hughes.
4. **Siddharth Chauhan** (DM, Brightmart) now reports to **Eleanor Hughes** (was Rahul Bhatia).
5. **Gaurav Kohli** (DM, Zentro Retail) continues to report to Rahul Bhatia.
6. **James Carter** (Client Partner, Brightmart) moves UKD → CON; manager unchanged (Eleanor Hughes).
