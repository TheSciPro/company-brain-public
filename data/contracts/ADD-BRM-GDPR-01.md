# Data Processing Addendum ADD-BRM-GDPR-01

**§1 Roles.** Northwind is the Processor; Brightmart is the Controller. Purpose: loyalty platform development, support and analytics.

**§3 Personal Data.** Loyalty member name, email, postcode, purchase history. No special-category data.

**§5 International transfer.** To India under the UK IDTA, with access only from approved ODC locations (Northwind Bangalore ODC-2, Floor 6) and Brightmart Citrix.

**§6 Sub-processors** require Brightmart's prior written consent.

**§8 Breach notification.** Processor shall notify the Brightmart DPO of a Personal Data Breach **within 24 hours of becoming aware** (stricter than the 72h regulator deadline under UK GDPR Art. 33). This clause prevails over any internal Processor procedure.

**§9 Audit.** Brightmart may audit annually with 30 days' notice.

**§11 Remote working.** Personnel processing Personal Data **must not work remotely** (see MSA §7.3).
