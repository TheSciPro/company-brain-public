# Master Services Agreement — Brightmart UK plc

**§1 Term.** Three (3) years from the Effective Date (2026-02-12), renewable by mutual agreement.

**§2.2 Order of precedence.** In case of conflict: (a) the Data Processing Addendum (ADD-BRM-GDPR-01) prevails on data-protection matters; (b) otherwise this MSA prevails over any SOW; (c) this Agreement prevails over the Supplier's internal policies for Supplier Personnel assigned to Brightmart.

**§5 Charges.** Per Schedule 3 (Rate Card RC-2026 as adjusted). _[Schedule 3 restricted: Finance, DM, Exec]_

**§7.3 Work location.** Supplier Personnel processing Brightmart Personal Data shall work **only from Supplier ODC premises (Bangalore ODC-2, Floor 6) or Brightmart offices. No home or remote working.**

**§9 Key personnel.** The Solution Architect is named Key Personnel. Replacement requires **thirty (30) days' prior written notice** and Brightmart's written approval.

**§12 Security incidents.** Supplier shall notify Brightmart of any security incident affecting Brightmart data **within twenty-four (24) hours** of becoming aware.

**§14 Service levels.** Service credits apply for SLA misses as set out in Schedule 4, capped at 10% of monthly charges.

**§16 Audit.** Brightmart may audit Supplier's compliance annually, and at any time following a Sev1 incident, and may require a remediation plan within 14 days.

**§19 Termination for cause** following repeated material breach of §7.3, §12 or the DPA.
