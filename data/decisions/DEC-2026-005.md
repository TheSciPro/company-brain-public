# DEC-2026-005: Pricing Committee: 18% discount with 21% floor margin and 2027 reprice checkpoint

## Status
Accepted

## Context
12% draft rejected by client; competitor ~20% lower; strategic logo.
## Decision
Recommend RC-2026 less **18%**, floor margin **21%** (vs 28% target), reprice checkpoint Feb 2027; exception valid for initial term only (renegotiate at 2029 renewal). >15% ⇒ CFO **and** CEO approval (APR-2026-0201, PEX-2026-004).
## Consequences
Margin below 22% requires quarterly recovery review (POL-FIN-PRICE §3.3).
