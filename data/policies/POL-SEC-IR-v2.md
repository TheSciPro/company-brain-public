# Security Incident Response Procedure (v2)

**2 Severity.** Sev1 — confirmed client data exposure; Sev2 — policy violation with potential exposure; Sev3 — no client data.

**3 Reporting.** Report to the SOC within 1 hour. The SOC opens a SEC-* ticket and notifies the IT Security Manager.

**4 Client data incidents.** Sev1/Sev2 involving client data: notify the DPO and DM immediately. **Client notification follows the contractual timeline (default 72h unless the contract specifies otherwise).**

**6 Post-incident review** due within 10 working days, with a corrective action plan (CAPA) tracked to closure.
