# Travel Policy (v3)

**3.1 Domestic.** Economy air if > 500 km. Hotels capped by city tier (Tier-1 ₹7,500/night for G1–G5, ₹11,000 for G6+).

**3.2 International.** Economy for flights < 8h; premium economy for > 8h at G6+ only. **Pre-approval by G6 + Unit Head (G8).** Client-billable travel needs written confirmation from the DM that the client will reimburse. Self-approval is never allowed (POL-FIN-AUTH v5).

**3.3 UK business visitor rules.** India-based staff travelling to London for client work **> 30 days** need an immigration review (HR + Legal) and **CHRO approval**.

**3.4 Per diem.** London £55/day.
