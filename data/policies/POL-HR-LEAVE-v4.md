# Leave Policy (v4)

**4.1 Earned / privilege leave.** India: 18 days/year, accrued 1.5/month, carry-forward up to 45 days. UK: 25 days plus bank holidays; carry-forward max 5 days, to be used by 31 March.

**4.2 Sick leave.** India: 10 days/year (medical certificate required beyond 2 consecutive days). UK: statutory sick pay plus 10 days company sick pay.

**4.3 Approval** (per POL-FIN-AUTH v5 §3 / DoA rows DOA-12..14): ≤5 working days — line manager; 6–15 days — line manager **and** the Project Manager if the employee is allocated to a billable project; >15 days or unpaid — HRBP + G6.

**4.4 Notice.** Employees on a client project must give **15 days' notice** for leave of more than 5 days, unless it is an emergency.

**4.5 Client blackout periods.** Client contracts may impose blackout periods; the contract prevails for the period stated. Known blackout: **Brightmart UK — no planned leave 2026-11-15 to 2026-12-31** (peak trading freeze, SOW-BRM-01 §5.2). Emergency leave remains available.
