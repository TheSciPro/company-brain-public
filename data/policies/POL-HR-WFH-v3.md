# Hybrid & Work-From-Home Policy (v3)

**2.1 India.** Up to **3 days/week** WFH. Minimum **2 anchor days** in office (Tue/Thu by default).

**2.2 UK.** Up to **3 days/week** WFH. Fully remote by exception with Unit Head approval.

**2.3 Work from anywhere.** New: up to **20 days/year** "work from anywhere" within the employee's country, with manager approval.

**2.4 Devices.** WFH is permitted only on **Northwind-managed, MDM-enrolled devices** connected via Northwind VPN. Personal devices are prohibited for any client data. (ISO A.6.7 / A.8.1.)

**2.5 Precedence.** Where a client contract, SOW or DPA specifies work location or device requirements, **the client requirement prevails** for staff allocated to that client, for the duration of the allocation. The DM must publish the override to the team. (Brightmart override: DEC-2026-014.)

**2.6 Changes from v2.** WFH days 2→3; work-from-anywhere added; device clause hardened.
