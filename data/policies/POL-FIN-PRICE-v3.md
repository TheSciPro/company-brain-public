# Pricing & Discount Policy (v3)

**3.1** Target gross margin 28% for T&M, 32% for fixed price.

**3.2** Discounts vs rate card: ≤10% DM; 10–15% Unit Head; **>15% CFO and CEO jointly** plus a Pricing Committee note recording the rationale and floor margin.

**3.3** Any deal below 22% margin needs a recovery plan reviewed quarterly by Finance.

**3.4** Exceptions are valid for the **initial contract term only** and must be renegotiated at renewal.
