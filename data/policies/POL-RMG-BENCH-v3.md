# Bench & Allocation Policy (v3)

_Changed because of the Coastline / Varun Shah release (ESC-2026-002, DEC-2026-017)._

**2.1** Bench = unallocated > 5 working days. RM may allocate bench staff to any approved demand.

**2.2** Release from an active project needs the releasing DM's **and** the releasing **Unit Head's** approval if handover is < 15 working days, **plus a check by Legal for key-person clauses**.

**2.4** New: a mid-project release must have a **named backfill** before the release date.

**2.5** Bench > **45 days** triggers a review (was 60).
